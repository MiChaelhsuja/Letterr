For Spring

a letter I should have written using my skills on developing

Hello, I just want to say that I'm really sorry for everything I've done that made you feel uncomfortable. I know there were times na naging unprofessional ako and I crossed boundaries that I should've respected from the start. I take full responsibility for that and I'm sincerely sorry po.

I also miss the Spring / Jhen na una kong nakilala, and I still remember the birthday letter you gave me back then. Sobrang na-appreciate ko talaga yon, and until now I still value all the effort, time and care na binigay mo sa akin. Ayoko ring isipin na nasayang or na-disappoint ko lahat ng efforts mo dahil sa mga naging actions ko.

I know saying sorry isn't enough, so this time I want to show it through my actions, kahit na malayo tayo sa isa't isa. I'll learn from my mistakes, set proper boundaries for myself, and respect any decision you make. I don't want to pressure you or make you feel like you owe me anything.

Kung okay lang sa'yo at komportable ka — pwede ba kitang ligawan ulit? Kahit ano pang sagot mo, i re respect ko po yan.

This time, I want to do things properly, slowly, and with more respect for you and your boundaries.

With all my respect, Ryu
